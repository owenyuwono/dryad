High succulence → swollen cactus body. Low → bark trunk. Bark: hue (0=grey, 1=warm brown/red), lightness (0=dark, 1=pale/birch), furrow density (0=few wide furrows → 1=many fine), plates (0=continuous ridges → oak, 1=discrete flaking plates). The vertical furrow RELIEF (grooves + normals + colour) is generated from the flow-line furrow field — see the "Furrow lines" render mode. Woodiness: 1=woody bark, 0=soft green herbaceous stem. Trunk height: 0.5=identity, 0=short bush, 1=tall. Trunk taper: 0=natural taper, 1=cylindrical trunk.
High branchiness → bushy; low → columnar or single-stem. Whorl: 0=continuous branching, 1=distinct tiered whorls (pine/fir). Droop bias negative → upswept; positive → weeping form. Weep: 0=no-op, 1=strongly pendulous.
Leaf division: 0=simple blade (a needle is this with a very narrow Leaf width), 1=compound/divided (frond); Frond fan morphs the leaflets feather↔fan. Tip tuft: 0=no-op, 1=foliage concentrated at branch tips.
